CollegeBook — The Digital Campus Network & Student Collaboration Hub | College-Book

A purpose-built digital campus platform for college and university students to connect, collaborate on projects, build hackathon teams, and preserve their college story.

Key Features of CollegeBook (College-Book)

  • Clean Campus Feed: Authentic student posts, campus discussions, and anonymous appreciation.
  • Cross-Campus Explore: Discover open-source projects, breakthroughs, and discussions across engineering colleges.
  • Collab Hub: Assemble hackathon squads, recruit developers, and collaborate in private team channels.
  • myCon Verified Skill Badges: Proof-backed technical credentials for competitive programming, web development, and machine learning.
  • Graduation Memory Book: Time-bound accounts that package all your memories and achievements upon graduation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does CollegeBook verify student identity?

Students register with institutional college emails or select their verified campus from our comprehensive database of engineering colleges.

Is CollegeBook free for students?

Yes, CollegeBook is 100% free and student-focused.
